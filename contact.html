💡 Tips for fast troubleshooting

When reporting an issue, including the following details helps resolve it quickly:

  • Windows Version: (e.g. Windows 11 23H2 64-bit)
  • Android Device Model & OS: (e.g. Samsung Galaxy S22 running Android 14)
  • Connection Type: USB Cable or Wireless ADB
  • The Specific Test Step: (e.g. failure during Tap "Checkout")
